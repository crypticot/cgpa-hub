Help
Everything the tool does, in one place

GPA (Grade Point Average) is your average performance in a single semester. CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is your running average across every semester you've completed so far.

CGPA isn't an average of your semester GPAs. It's a weighted total: every quality point you've earned, divided by every unit you've taken, across your whole record. A semester with more units carries more weight in that total.

Each course you add has a unit (credit) value and a grade. Every grade is worth a number of points, set in Settings → Grading Scale.

Quality points for a course = units × the grade's points.

GPA for a semester = total quality points in that semester ÷ total units in that semester.

CGPA = total quality points across every semester ÷ total units across every semester.

Example — Year 1, 1st Semester:
MTH 101, 4 units, grade A (5.0 points) → 4 × 5 = 20 quality points
MEE 101, 3 units, grade B (4.0 points) → 3 × 4 = 12 quality points
Total: 7 units, 32 quality points → GPA = 32 ÷ 7 = 4.57

Go to Course Records and fill in the course, year, semester, units and grade. The course field takes anything, a code, a title, or both together.

Use the two dropdowns above the table to narrow it down, say, to just Year 3, 1st Semester. Selecting one narrows the other to combinations that actually exist in your data.

Click a column header to sort by it. Click it again to flip direction, and a third time to clear that sort. Use Clear sort to go back to the original order at any time.

Click the pencil icon on any row to edit that entry, or the trash icon to delete it. If you started editing by mistake, the Cancel button beside the form returns it to Add mode.

If you took a course more than once, add it again as a new entry. Both attempts count toward your CGPA. If your school only counts one, delete the entry you don't want counted.

The trajectory chart plots your GPA for every semester in order, with no filter, so you can see your overall direction.

The cards above the course chart adjust to whatever you've filtered the chart to. Filter to one semester and you'll see the GPA for just that semester, plus your CGPA up to that point. Clear the filter and they show your overall GPA and CGPA instead. Best and weakest semester always look at your full record, since comparing semesters only makes sense across all of them.

The course chart compares individual courses within your current filter. Sort it highest to lowest to see your strongest courses first, or reset it back to the order you entered them in.

Not every school runs the same way, so everything is editable. In Settings you can rename or add years, rename or add semesters, and change the grading scale entirely, add, remove, or rename grades, and change what each is worth.

You can also edit the Classification Bands, the names (First Class, Second Class Upper, and so on) and the minimum CGPA each one requires. Change the grade points to match a 4.0 scale and set the bands to your school's published cutoffs, and every label across the app updates itself.

If you try to remove a year, semester or grade that's still used by a course, the tool will stop you and tell you which entries are using it. Update or delete those first.

Your work saves automatically in this browser. To move it elsewhere or keep a backup, use Save Project to download a file, and Load Project to bring it back in on any device.

Reset clears everything, courses and settings, back to a blank slate. It asks you to confirm first, since it can't be undone.
